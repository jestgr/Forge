Forge and your health data

Forge is a personal workout app. It reads health data from Health Connect (steps, sleep, calories, distance, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, weight, body fat and workouts) to show it next to your workouts and count it toward your weekly goals.

The data stays on this phone, in Forge's own storage. Forge has no server, sends it nowhere, and never writes to Health Connect. It leaves the phone only in a backup file that you save yourself.

You can remove Forge's access at any time in Health Connect settings.
